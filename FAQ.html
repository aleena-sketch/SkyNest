Before your stay

Getting started

SkyNest is designed for young professionals, digital nomads, international students and others looking for flexible accommodation. Custom arrangements may also be available for corporate clients.

SkyNest offers Premium Stay, Standard Stay, Short-Term Stay and Custom Packages. Each option provides different accommodation features and levels of access to shared facilities.

Short-Term Stay offers flexible bookings from one to four weeks. Other packages can support different stay requirements. Contact SkyNest to discuss your intended length of stay.

Extensions can be discussed with the SkyNest team depending on availability and your accommodation arrangement. Contact the team before your current booking ends to discuss your options.

Accommodation details

Packages and rooms

Yes. SkyNest provides furnished accommodation, allowing residents to settle in without needing to arrange all their own furniture.

Premium Stay provides a private studio apartment, weekly cleaning and access to all facilities. Standard Stay provides a private bedroom in a shared apartment, fortnightly cleaning and access to shared spaces such as the coworking area, kitchen and lounge.

Short-Term Stay includes a furnished room, utilities and limited access to shared facilities. It is designed for flexible stays lasting between one and four weeks.

Yes. Utilities are included in the Premium, Standard and Short-Term packages listed on this website. Custom arrangements can be discussed with the SkyNest team.

Cleaning is included in selected packages. Premium Stay includes weekly cleaning, while Standard Stay includes fortnightly cleaning. Please check your selected package for its specific arrangements.

Living at SkyNest

Facilities and services

High-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the building. Internet access is also included in the Premium Stay package.

Yes. SkyNest provides shared coworking spaces for residents who need an area to study, work remotely or complete daily tasks. Access depends on the selected package.

Shared kitchen and dining areas are available as part of the co-living facilities. Access depends on the facilities included in your selected package.

Facilities include a gym, yoga studio, rooftop garden, social lounge, study pods, laundry facilities and communal spaces. Access may vary depending on the selected package.

SkyNest includes 24/7 security and key-card access as part of its accommodation facilities.
